# Limaraya — Rumah untuk cerita yang belum selesai

Eight-page original Indonesian illustrated fiction about Nara, Bima and Sari restoring a village library and learning to ask for help. All characters and places are fictional. Not a company biography.

- PDF retains the public sample URL `example/limarayamusic.pdf`.
- Text/layout source: `scripts/story.py` (Python + ReportLab). Rebuild from the repository root.
- Three original illustrations made with the built-in imagegen tool, then optimized as JPEG document assets. PDF contains selectable text; pages are not flattened screenshots.
- Warm hand-painted animation / watercolor atmosphere, Indonesian village architecture, moss green, cream and gold. No embedded illustration text/logos.

## Illustration prompt set

1. Cover: three Indonesian young adult friends (short dark bob woman in moss-green cardigan, round-glasses man in ochre shirt, long-braided woman in terracotta dress) approach a wooden library beneath a giant banyan after rain. Lush rural village, golden sun, whimsical hand-painted Japanese animation background, watercolor/gouache, quiet friendship, landscape, no text.
2. Rain: the same friends save water-damaged books inside the library during a tropical storm. Leaking roof, bucket, lantern, small green bird at the window. Hopeful teamwork, natural expressions, warm green/amber/cream palette, landscape, no text.
3. Evening: the restored library at golden twilight. Friends read aloud to village children on the veranda; repaired roof, lanterns, blank paper flags, green bird overhead, rice paddies and misty mountains. Tender watercolor/gouache animation atmosphere, landscape, no text.

The sample demonstrates book/single/webtoon/manga layouts. The Indonesian story itself uses normal left-to-right text; manga changes page-turn direction, not text direction or PDF numbering.
